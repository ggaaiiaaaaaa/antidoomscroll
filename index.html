AntiDoomscroll — Project Proposal

A mobile application designed to break the mindless habit of short-form video scrolling (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts).

  • Active Pauses: Instead of simple dismissible timers, it introduces a breathing pause, an intention reflection, or a physical check (like 5 squats).
  • Redirection: Offers 1-tap shortcuts to switch to reading (Kindle), study tasks (Notion), or a bedtime wind-down.
  • Privacy: Operates entirely on the device without uploading camera or activity history.

Permanent Anti-Bypass Protection

Enabled automatically upon installation: AntiDoomscroll uses Android's official Device Administrator API to eliminate deletion bypasses.

  • Deletion Blocked: Android's system "Uninstall" button is permanently disabled by DevicePolicyManager.
  • Task Incompletion Lock: If a physical challenge or scroll pause is pending, bypassing via app deletion is completely impossible.
  • Settings Shield: The background Accessibility Service detects any attempt to open Android Settings to force-close the app and immediately redirects back to the task.
